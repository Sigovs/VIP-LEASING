Service

The car is the start of the relationship, not the end of it. What happens after you drive it away is arranged here, and accounted for.

What we look after

Scheduled maintenance

Factory-interval work booked, tracked and reported back to you. We hold the history so the car's file stays whole — which is what it is worth on the day you sell it.

Detailing and paint protection

Correction, ceramic coating and film, specified per car rather than per package. A wrapped nose on a car that never sees a highway is money spent in the wrong place.

Pre-purchase inspection

Found a car somewhere else? We will look at it the way we look at one we are buying ourselves, and tell you what we find — including when the answer is to walk away.

Storage

Climate-controlled, battery tended, started and moved on a schedule. Collected and returned when you want it, rather than when the unit is open.

Transport and delivery

Enclosed, insured, and to your door — the same way the cars we sell arrive. Anywhere in Florida as standard, and further when it is worth the trip.

How it runs

1

Tell us the car

What it is, what it needs, and when you want it back. If it is a car we sold you, we already have the file.

2

We quote it

One number, itemised, before anything is touched. If the work turns out to be smaller than the estimate, the invoice is smaller too.

3

You approve

Nothing proceeds without a yes. If something else surfaces on the lift, it comes back to you as a separate question — never as a line you find later.

4

Collected and returned

We can take the car and bring it back, washed and with the paperwork. You do not have to see the inside of a workshop unless you want to.

The workshop

Work is carried out by marque specialists we place cars with, not in a general bay. We book it, we follow it, and you get one invoice and one point of contact — ours.

Drop-off is at the showroom, Miami, or we collect.
